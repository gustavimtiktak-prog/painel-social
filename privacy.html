Privacy Policy

Painel Social · Last updated: November 14, 2026

Painel Social ("the app") is a scheduling and publishing tool that lets a creator post their own videos and photos to their own Instagram and TikTok accounts and see the posts already published on those accounts. This policy explains what data the app uses and how.

1. Data we access

Only after you connect an account and authorize the app, it accesses:

  • TikTok (with the scopes you approve: user.info.basic, user.info.profile, user.info.stats, video.list, video.publish): your display name, username, avatar, follower/following/like/video counts, the list of your public videos with their basic statistics, and the permission to publish videos you choose to your account.
  • Instagram: your username, profile picture, follower counts, the list of your posts with likes, comments and views, and the permission to publish the content you choose.
  • Content you upload: the videos, images, captions and cover images you add to a post.

2. How we use it

  • To publish the content you select, to the accounts you select, at the time you choose.
  • To show you which account will receive a post (name and avatar) and your account's posting options (privacy levels, comment/duet/stitch settings, maximum video length).
  • To show your published posts and their statistics inside the app.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling or any other purpose, and we never post anything without your explicit action.

3. Where data is stored

The app runs on your own computer. Access tokens, account settings, scheduled posts and uploaded files are stored locally on that computer. They are not sent to any server operated by us. Data is exchanged only with the official TikTok and Instagram (Meta) APIs. When a file has to be fetched by Instagram, the app opens a temporary, randomly named link that serves only that file and closes right after the upload.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your data with third parties. Content is sent only to the platform you choose to publish to, under that platform's own terms and privacy policy.

5. Retention and deletion

  • Remove an account in the app (Contas → Remover) to delete its tokens and settings from your computer.
  • Delete scheduled posts in the app at any time. Uploaded files are stored only on your computer and can be deleted there.
  • You can revoke the app's access at any time in TikTok (Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions) and in Instagram/Meta (Settings → Apps and websites).

6. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country to use TikTok and Instagram).

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.

8. Contact

Questions or requests: gustavimtiktak@gmail.com

O Painel Social é uma ferramenta para o criador agendar e publicar os próprios vídeos e fotos nas próprias contas do Instagram e do TikTok e ver os posts já publicados.

Dados acessados, só depois que você conecta e autoriza a conta:

  • No TikTok: nome, @, foto, números do perfil, lista dos seus vídeos com estatísticas e permissão para publicar o que você escolher.
  • No Instagram: os mesmos tipos de dado.
  • Os arquivos e legendas que você envia.

Uso: os dados servem só para:

  • publicar o que você escolher, onde e quando você escolher;
  • mostrar a conta de destino e as opções de postagem;
  • exibir seus posts publicados.

Nada é publicado sem ação sua, e nada é usado para publicidade.

Armazenamento: o app roda no seu computador. Tokens, agendamentos e arquivos ficam salvos nele, e não em servidores nossos. Os dados trafegam só com as APIs oficiais do TikTok e do Instagram. Quando o Instagram precisa baixar um arquivo, o app abre um link temporário com nome aleatório, que serve apenas aquele arquivo e fecha logo depois.

Compartilhamento: não vendemos nem compartilhamos dados com terceiros.

Exclusão: remova a conta no app para apagar os tokens. Você também pode revogar o acesso a qualquer momento nas configurações do TikTok e do Instagram.
